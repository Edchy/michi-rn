## What this obliges

1. **Keep the credit visible in the app.** Currently the Credits footer on the Codex
   page (`src/app/codex/page.tsx`), which names Kia Leng Koh and links to the site.
2. **Keep the link.** Credit text without a working link does not meet the terms.
3. **Re-ask before any commercial use.** The grant was given for a personal project.

If any of these cannot be honored, replace the audio with a source whose terms fit
the way Michi is being distributed.
